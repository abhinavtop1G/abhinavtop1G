### Selected work

Each one opens its case study on my portfolio: the problem, the method, what
broke, and what the number does not show.

| | Project | What it is | Result |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| 01 | [**Real-Time UPI Scam Detection**](https://abhinav-saini-portfolio.vercel.app/#/projects/upi-scam-detection) | A payer-side session GRU and a payee-side XGBoost risk model, meeting at one alert decision | **60.9 ms** p95 end to end · **89.4%** of scams stopped (simulated) |
| 02 | [**BarrierX**](https://abhinav-saini-portfolio.vercel.app/#/projects/barrierx) | DeBERTa-v3 that ranks safety reports by what could have happened, served on ONNX Runtime | **0.80** precision@20 on held-out narratives |
| 03 | [**VeriFEarth**](https://abhinav-saini-portfolio.vercel.app/#/projects/verifearth) | Carbon-credit claims read by DistilBERT and checked against Sentinel-2 imagery | **0.95** F1, held-out |
| 04 | [**Kaggle Playground S6E8**](https://abhinav-saini-portfolio.vercel.app/#/projects/kaggle-s6e8) | 691K rows, where whether a value was missing was the signal | **0.96561** OOF AUC, 3-model blend |
| 05 | [**Real-Time Vehicle Detection**](https://abhinav-saini-portfolio.vercel.app/#/projects/edge-vehicle-detection) | YOLO detection and tracking on live camera feeds | Real time, on the device |
